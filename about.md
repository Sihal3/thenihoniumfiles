## The Author, Nihal Gulati

I'm a swash-buckley junior in high-school who's big into technology and science and math. You'll see me wax philosophical about these topics as well as whatever cool stuff I happen to be doing in my regular life.

Apart from slogging through the bog of work that is junior year, I write articles for the Octagon, my school's newspaper, help maintain their site, act as pretrial attorney on my school's Mock Trial Team, and generally code around. If, by some miracle, I have free time, I'll spend it reading a book or playing video games. Oh, and somewhere in all that I find time to post here.
